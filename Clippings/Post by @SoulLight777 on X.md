"Survival of the fittest" is a Reptilian ideology because it is rooted in competition, domination, exploitation, and the abandonment of those who appear weaker or less fortunate.

From our perspective, this is not the natural law of a spiritually evolved civilization; it is a lower consciousness system built upon fear, separation, and scarcity.

True evolution does not mean the strong crushing the weak. It means the strong becoming protectors, healers, teachers, and upliftors of the whole.

A higher civilization understands that every being carries inherent value and that the advancement of one should contribute to the advancement of all.

The higher way is not survival of the fittest, but elevation of the whole. Love does not abandon the vulnerable; it strengthens them, protects them, and helps them rise.

The shift from competition to cooperation is therefore not weakness, it is a sign of spiritual evolution.(Please repost)
