Someone said, "If you don't fight for your love, there is no love." It is a compelling phrase, but not a truthful one. Love, by its very nature, requires no struggle. A fight begins where the ego begins, and where the ego exists, love has already receded. Love is not conquest but allowance. If both the lover and the beloved are willing, there is nothing to fight for; the interaction unfolds of its own accord. The battle is not for love, it is for possession, pride, or resistance. A lover has no time to fight. Fight is for the absence of love.
