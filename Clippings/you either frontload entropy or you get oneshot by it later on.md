this applies to basically everything in life and is one of the esoteric secrets of thermodynamics once you understand it philosophically

entropy is not the enemy, thermodynamics is entirely beyond the realm of good and evil and is just an objective physics framework for understanding how energy transformation and stuff works

complex systems are most sensitive to their initial conditions

what is a complex system?

\- a storm is a complex system

\- a planet is a complex system

\- a living being is a complex system

\- a relationship is a complex system

\- a family is a complex system

\- a city is a complex system

\- a neural network is a complex system

\- a company is a complex system

many things are complex systems, etc

it might sound counterintuitive that it would be an advantage to frontload entropy

but it makes sense when you truly think about it

to frontload entropy means to import entropy from the future and export negentropy into the future

frontloading entropy thus creates far more stable initial conditions eventually by minimizing future entropic surprise

the more uncertainty you can embrace initially, the more certainty you’ll have later

in other words - the more you avoid uncertainty initially, the more fragile you’ll be later

the more uncertainty you consume, the more chaos you embrace without collapsing - the more knowledge, control, and stability you eventually gain

a relationship that deals with all their shit early on creates trust and stability if they get through it - a relationship that suppresses their problems creates a powder keg that will one day explode or lead to cheating or so on

a business that handles all their uncertainty and weaknesses and potential issues straight away creates a solid foundation for scale

thus they live their lives inverted to how they actually should be living - which is the same reason why so many men have mid life crises and become narcissistic and exploitative in their later years

a failure of embodying the wild vitality of youth while young leads to sour grapes in old age
