God's core lesson of which he is always trying to teach/remind you is 'do what you can, with what you have, and i am waiting'. It is this single instruction that follows you everywhere that you go - regardless of level, regardless of location, regardless of age, regardless of anything external. He waits for you when you stray, he reminds & guides you when you can't see, he encourages you as you suffer.

If you walk forth, more is given. And to walk forth you do not need to 'worry about what else to add, or what else to do'. You attack what you can, with what you have. And you ignore anything that isn't that, or is beyond that. Your FAITH is what is being cultivated, as you face your own divine flavor or suffering, stare it in the eyes, and say "i'm ready for more". You stop running away, and you start running towards. Sometimes you will feel invincible and like you can't miss, and other times you will feel like it may be more than you can handle, or that you've put too much on your plate, or bitten off more than you can chew. But that is the line of your expansion as a man. That is when you see the edge of the current rubber band, and CHOOSE to stretch it even further.

He who requests hunger will always be fed. But in order to SWALLOW what you say you want (and have it become you), you first need to be crafted/carved into the man who can handle what you say you want. When you ask for the capacity, God gives it to you. Oh, he will fucking give it to you. The question is - can you REMEMBER what you asked for you? Can you REMEMBER you asked for the FLOWER, when balls-deep in the RAIN?

Your 'small conviction' may waiver, but your 'large conviction' remains absolutely & completely unbreakable. It is weaved into stone. And step by step you pursue your god-given calling, walking forth into the darkness against monsters you cannot see or predict, with tools inadequate, but a heart + courage that can dominate & overcome anything that stands against it.

For your enemies & their shadows may have wordly tools...

But YOU have GOD.
