the secret of life is that it's uncertain and unpredictable. That's also what makes it beautiful. yet most men spend their lives trying to eliminate that uncertainty. They approach life like an equation to be solved, convinced that if they calculate every move, they'll eventually gain control.

Instead, they end up with the worst possible existence. a life spent optimizing rather than living. Then, when reality inevitably refuses to follow the script, they blame everything except the assumption that life was ever meant to be controlled in the first place.

It's fear that makes you calculative, not wisdom. Get rid of the fear, and clarity will do what calculation never could.

On a long enough timespan, life reveals itself to be a paradox. It's not that truth is relative or anything as shallow as this, but more like different angles of it become more salient in different contexts.

###### So to the binary mind, there is contradiction, and the assumption is that contradiction requires refutation to restore coherence. But it doesn't, because the contradiction is contextual, often cyclical, and reconcilable once you understand the hidden thread which binds (and thereby reveals) the relationship between superficial opposites.

This is paradox as depth, rather than as error.
