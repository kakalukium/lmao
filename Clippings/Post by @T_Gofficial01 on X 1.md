Showing cunning too early awaken fear, envy and resistance in the minds of others. The world does not attack the weak , it attacks what poses as threat. And you showing you know ‘too much’ can elicit unfavorable response from the ignorants and the insecured. So, sometimes, the best strategy is the opposite. Appear simple, speak less. Agree when it’s convenient. Let them underestimate you. While they laugh, you observe, while they expose themselves, you calculate. The man who appears foolish moves through the game unnoticed but the intelligent one who needs to prove everything all the time ends up surrounded by enemies. Words to the wise.
