Remember the videos of Indians drowning in shallow pools while their loved ones and friends fail to render aid or react at all?

Dr. Sukanthini "Suki" Subbiah is charged in Florida with aggravated manslaughter for standing at a pool's edge and watching a child in her care drown for 5 minutes. She then entered the water but did not attempt a rescue.

The child remained underwater for seven minutes before a bystander pulled him out. Unsurprisingly, the child died.

This Indian woman is a practicing hematologist and oncologist in America.
