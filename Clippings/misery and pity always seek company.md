the only people who will try to stop you from winning in any aspect of life are the ones who are beaten

misery and pity always seek company

you see it is the natural instinctive response of the pitiful to seek comfort by pulling others down to their level

this is what a ‘degenerative spirit’ truly is - it is the scarcity driven instinct which finds its fuel in fear and envy

the ‘degenerative spirit’ isn’t necessarily the physical condition it is often depicted as, although it often bleeds out into physical habits as it is a deeper infliction

it may sometimes manifest physically in appearance, but physiognomy is not rule - appearances can often deceive and thus to truly gauge someone’s spirit, only their behavioral economics and instinctual responses will reveal this

it is in fact often the case that the degenerative spirit will compensate by hyper-fixating on appearance, as that is how the weak soul often finds its cover and its route of least resistance for the easiest way through life

again however, appearances are not law

only instinct and unconscious behavior will tell you the truth

so if we look to the other polarity, then what is the ‘generative spirit’?

it is the vital spirit which is the creative and generative spirit, as creation and generation are one in the same

the soul of vitality is the soul of the artist

it all starts in the spirit

everything is art if you know how to see the magic and how to paint the world

it is the supreme pleasure of the vital spirit to find itself closest to God, because the creative forces find themselves in an entangled dance with the creator - as to create is to become closer to the creator

that is the true essence of vitality

and perhaps we all have both potential states within us - the potential to generate or degenerate the world around us

as spirit is the ontological aspect of life that itself cuts into life

thus you will even feel it in yourself if you reflect and pay attention

\- creativity is the graceful overflow of the vital state of spirit

\- impotence is the fearful scarcity of the degenerative state of spirit

when the vital spirit is the primary state then you will find yourself into natural overflow, seeking to inspire others without any form of payment in return, naturally creating and naturally embodying the artistic, playful, and potent aspects of self

and vice versa, when in the degenerative state you will find yourself oozing with insecurity and envy, gossiping and spreading rumors, fearful of what others might have or attain, looking for a transaction of value in everything, always feeling cheated and beaten

pay attention to yourself

pay attention to others

vitality inspires vitality as degeneration inspires degeneration

seek the romantics to enchant your life, and stay away from the sticklers and gossipers lest you want to watch yourself become small like them

as vitality in its truest form is a romance with existence, just like how degeneration is a nihilism

the nihilist competes and is obsessed with pulling down others - a gossiping little ghoul that whispers and spreads poison in order to find itself more sweet

the romantic recognizes competition as scarcity and fear - learning creation, inspiration, and art as the highest state of being, creating not to compete, but creating out of romantic passion for life

‘beaten’ is abstract here btw - it doesn’t mean surface level beaten, it means spiritually beaten

someone can have an extreme amount of material success, but still be beaten in spirit

most won’t understand because of surface level shenanigans, but if you have a piercing spiritual sight you’ll know what I mean
