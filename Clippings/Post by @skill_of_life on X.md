The quickest way to destroy your peace is by being around arrogant people. They don't listen, they dismiss, and they thrive on making others feel small.

Their presence turns every interaction into a battle for dominance. You'll find yourself drained, irritated, and questioning why you even tolerate it.

Peace doesn't survive in that environment. Cut them out, keep your distance, and protect your space from people who think they're above everyone else.
