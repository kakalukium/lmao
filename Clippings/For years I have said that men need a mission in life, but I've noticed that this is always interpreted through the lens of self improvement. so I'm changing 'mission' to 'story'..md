 For years I have said that men need a mission in life, but I've noticed that this is always interpreted through the lens of self improvement.

When that's not what I mean, so I'm changing 'mission' to 'story'.

This will get you out of your head and into your body.

Every man needs to be crafting an epic story for his life, and this includes his romantic life.

He needs grand quests, and a turbulent plot taking place in his life, that he has created, and that his 'Hedoness' feels deeply a part of.

This crafts the best relationships between a man and a woman.

Your story needs ups and downs, tension and release, pain and pleasure, etc etc.

The Cultured Hedonist archetype crafts that by seeking out things that he's called to and that make his palms sweat and his heart race.

(Painting by Charles Napier Kennedy, titled: Perseus and Andromeda)
