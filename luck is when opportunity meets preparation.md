IF YOU HAVE ADHD, PLEASE LEARN ABOUT LIMERENCE BEFORE IT RUINS YOUR RELATIONSHIPS.

It’s not "finding your soulmate." It is a severe, involuntary dopamine addiction masquerading as romance.

---

Limerence is an obsessive, intrusive romantic attachment. Because ADHD brains live in a chronic dopamine deficit, a new crush delivers a massive neurochemical high that completely hijacks your brain's reward center.

---

Your executive function locks onto this person the exact same way it locks onto a new hyperfixation. The thoughts become 24/7, intrusive, and impossible to voluntarily redirect.

---

Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD) aggressively twists every interaction. A delayed text feels like total abandonment; a casual compliment feels like a lifelong commitment.

---

The real danger: Limerence makes you destroy healthy, stable relationships in pursuit of chaotic high-dopamine connections that are completely unsustainable long-term.

---

Understanding limerence won't instantly cure the obsession, but it separates the neurological glitch from reality. You stop making life-altering choices based on a dopamine spike.
