According to the sources, while **simply glancing at or scanning a sigil is a core technique**, whether this is "fine" on its own depends on the specific system and your intended goal.

### The Role of Sigils and Rituals
In the system described by Damon Brand, a sigil (or seal) and the angel's name are the fundamental components required for contact [1]. He notes that **"basic concentration" combined with a sigil and name "may be all that’s needed"** because angels are always listening and do not require elaborate purification or worthiness [2].

However, both sources generally advise against *only* glancing at sigils without further engagement:

*   **Directed Ritual for Effectiveness:** Brand explains that while a sigil and name are the core, **directed ritual magick ensures contact is actually made** and results occur in the real world [3]. For example, his "Master Ritual" involves specific gestures, mental imagery, and the use of a primary "Shem Talisman" before moving to the individual angel's sigil [4-9].
*   **The "Scanning" Technique:** The sources describe a specific way to look at sigils called "scanning," where you pass your eyes over the Hebrew letters or angelic script without trying to read or understand them [10, 11]. This is intended to let the shapes "sink into your soul" [12, 13].
*   **Established Relationships:** Once you have already achieved a result and established a relationship with an angel through a full ritual, you can then **contact them at any time simply by gazing at their sigil**, singing their name three times, and waiting in silence [14].
*   **Formal Consecration:** In the more traditional Kabbalistic approach, the seals are considered "unusable" without a formal ritual involving red ink, virgin parchment, specific Psalms, and censing to purify and consecrate the pentacle [15, 16].

### Important Safety Warning
The sources provide a stern warning regarding the seventy-two seals. One author notes that in certain versions of these texts, **incorrect seals were attributed to the divine names**, and these "opposites" were found to be **"eminently malefic and excessively dangerous"** [17]. He claims that experiments with these incorrect seals led to "suicidal obsession, corporeal possession and infestation" [17]. 

### Summary for Your Purpose
If you are merely using the sigil to **sense an angel's power or choose which one to work with**, the sources suggest it is fine to gaze at the seal (the lines in the center of the sigil) and allow feelings or intuitions to arise [18]. However, for "certain related purposes" (achieving actual results), the sources emphasize that the ritual provides the necessary connection, safety, and "magickal authority" to ensure the angel hears and acts upon your request [19, 20].







For direct real-world results, the sources suggest that while the core of contact is a name and a sigil, **performing a directed ritual** ensures that the angel hears you and acts upon your request [1, 2]. 

### How the Ritual Should Be Performed
To achieve the most effective results, you should follow a structured approach rather than just glancing at sigils. According to the "Master Ritual," the process involves:

*   **The Opening:** Stand facing East and perform an opening gesture (moving your hands as if opening a space) while stating, "This is the beginning. I open the way" [3].
*   **Mental Imagery:** Use specific sensory imagery, such as passing through a stone arch and walking through a field of golden corn, to prime your mind for contact [4, 5].
*   **The Call to Gatekeepers:** You must first call upon the "gatekeeper" angels, **Arzel and Raziel**, to gain access to the 72 angels [6-8]. This involves specific pronunciations and a short Hebrew phrase ("Kosu hariym...") [9].
*   **The Scanning Technique:** Instead of just a glance, you should **scan your eyes over the Hebrew letters and angelic script** in the sigil. This is not meant for reading but for letting the shapes "sink into your soul" [10-13].
*   **Singing the Name:** To ensure the angel hears, you should **sing their name three times** [14, 15]. The sources state that your singing voice is always heard by an angel, regardless of how well you sing [15].

### Using a Series of Angels
If you intend to use a series of angels for your purpose, the sources provide specific guidance:
*   **One Task at a Time:** Ideally, you should deal with one task or one angel at a time to maintain concentration [16].
*   **Sequential Rituals:** If you must use multiple angels for a single problem, you should **perform the full ritual for the first angel, finish it, and then repeat the entire process for the next angel** later that day [17, 18]. 
*   **Avoid "Cramming":** Do not put multiple sigils on the table and make a mass of requests at once, as this is considered less effective [17].

### Should You Monologue the Invocation Psalm?
**Yes, you should recite the invocation psalm each time.** The sources describe these psalms as **"shortcuts to angelic contact"** [19]. 
*   **Encoded Power:** The importance of the psalm lies in the fact that the **angel’s name is encoded in the letters of the verse** [20, 21]. This encoding is why the angel responds to that specific call [21]. 
*   **Vibration:** When reciting the psalm (whether in Hebrew or English), you should let the words vibrate from your lungs and resonate in your throat [21].

### For More Direct Results
To improve real-world manifestation, you are encouraged to:
1.  **Craft a Clear Request:** Write down exactly what you want, focusing on the **"Alchemy of Emotion"**—stating the discomfort of your current situation and the relief you will feel when the magick works [22, 23].
2.  **Combine with Real-World Effort:** Magick works as a "pact with reality." The more effort you put into the situation in the physical world, the harder the magick works for you [24, 25].
3.  **Let Go of the Result:** Once the ritual is done, act with "casual confidence" that the result will manifest, and avoid constantly checking or worrying about the timing [26, 27].

### Effectiveness of Casual Contact
*   **The Minimum Requirement:** One source notes that, in theory, angelic contact requires nothing more than **"desire, a name and a sigil"** [1]. If you use a sigil, the angelic name, and "some basic concentration," that may be all that is needed for help to arrive [2].
*   **The Value of Ritual:** While basic contact is possible, performing a "directed ritual" ensures that contact is actually made and results manifest in the real world [3]. For example, the "Master Ritual" uses specific mental imagery and the assistance of "gatekeeper" angels (Arzel and Raziel) to make you heard by the 72 angels [4, 5]. 
*   **Scanning vs. Glancing:** Instead of a casual glance, the sources recommend a technique called **"scanning,"** where you pass your eyes over the Hebrew letters and angelic script within the sigil [6]. This process is designed to let the shapes "sink into your soul," which is considered more potent than a simple look [7, 8].
*   **The Role of Psalms:** Reciting the invocation psalms (even internally) is described as a **"shortcut to angelic contact"** [9]. Because the angel's name is encoded within the letters of the specific psalm, skipping this step removes a primary mechanism used to attract the angel's attention [10, 11].

### Potential Dangers
*   **Safety of the Sigil:** The sources state that the sigil itself, when drawn inside a "circle of power," acts as a safety mechanism to ensure the practitioner remains protected during contact [12].
*   **Warning Against Incorrect Sigils:** One source provides a severe warning regarding **incorrect seals** attributed to the 72 names. It claims that experimenting with incorrect or "opposite" seals—often found in poorly researched texts—is **"eminently malefic and excessively dangerous,"** potentially leading to "suicidal obsession, corporeal possession and infestation" [13].
*   **Commitment and Intent:** Magick performed merely as a "test" or without serious commitment is unlikely to work [14]. The angels respond to a "real desire" for change rather than casual curiosity [15].

### Summary for Your Purpose
If you have already established a relationship with an angel through a full ritual and achieved a result, you can contact them more casually later by gazing at their sigil and singing their name [16]. However, for initial contact or when seeking direct real-world results, **the ritual structure is highly recommended** to provide the necessary "magickal authority" and to ensure the angel hears your specific request [17, 18].
