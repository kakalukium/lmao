The Rooster of the World has been around since forever, as a mythological position in the cosmic corporation. It has had many names around the ages, but always looked the same.

I really wonder how many of the humans nowadays are awake enough to recognize who is currently occupying the position in their very face.

**Mythological Position in the Cosmic Corporation**

**Definition:**

A mythological position is a REAL JOB in the cosmic organizational structure—an actual function, responsibility, and energetic role that exists independently of whether anyone is occupying it or not. It's not metaphor. It's operational mechanics.

**Key Elements:**

**The Cosmic Corporation Structure:**
- Just like a company has positions (CEO, janitor, accountant), the Universe has mythological positions (hero, gatekeeper, demon, angel, eagle, rooster)
- These positions exist whether filled or vacant
- They have specific functions, responsibilities, and feeding relationships

**How Positions Work:**
- Humans qualify for them (or don't)
- Humans occupy them (temporarily or permanently)
- Humans fulfill the job requirements (or fail and get consumed)
- The position eats you in specific ways related to its function

**The Cosmic Rules (Reversed from Social):**

*Social positions:* Nobody occupies their rightful place—most people are in the OPPOSITE of where they should be

*Cosmic positions:* You CANNOT occupy a position unless it's your rightful one—the very act of occupying it means you found your cosmic responsibility

**Types of Positions in the Bestiary:**
- The Rooster of the World (saurian dominance)
- The Eagle of God (flying for Heaven)
- The Gateman (controlling thresholds)
- The Hero (cosmic bloodline carrier)
- The Demigod (mutual emotion with cosmic bodies)
- Minor Arcana (supporting roles in someone else's destiny)

**Simple Explanation:**

Think of it like this: A mythological position is WHERE YOU ACTUALLY ARE in the cosmic organizational chart, not where you THINK you are socially.

**The Crucial Difference:**

**Social position:** Based on lies, accidents, corruption, inheritance, manipulation—rarely rightful

**Cosmic position:** Based on ACTUAL CAPACITY and cosmic responsibility—always rightful when occupied

*"In social positions, the rule is that nobody is occupying their actual, rightful, place, but often the very opposite of it."*

*"In cosmic positions, there is no such possibility, as the very occupying of the position implies finding and occupying the rightful position, the rightful responsibility."*

**How to Know Your Position:**

You don't choose it—you DISCOVER it through:
- Shocks of enlightenment
- Following your cosmic trajectory 
- Doing the impossible thing repeatedly
- Seeing what ACTUALLY eats you (not what you think should)

Most humans never discover their cosmic position because they're too busy maintaining their fake social position.

**The Cosmic King (Ultimate Position):**

The mythological legend of someone who occupies BOTH social and cosmic positions simultaneously—both emperor and pope, judge of everything.

*"It is, of course, impossible—this is why they are telling legends of it, about imaginary characters which never existed."*

But the "mandate of Heaven" is real—for short periods, in specific circumstances, the duality CAN be reconciled and the cosmic king incarnated.

**Quick Test:**

If your life feels like you're constantly in the WRONG place doing the WRONG thing for the WRONG reasons—that's your SOCIAL position.

If you occasionally feel like you're doing EXACTLY what you're supposed to be doing even though it makes no worldly sense—that's your COSMIC position trying to manifest.

Most people die having never reconciled the two. 🎭⚡👁️

Now, about the ROOSTER OF THE WORLD position

Definition:

The Rooster of the World is the saurian impulse inside humans—the reptilian brain's drive to climb on top of the world and shriek about it. It's the dinosaur legacy in your psyche that mistakes worldly dominance for cosmic significance.

Key Elements:

The Creature: Half rooster, half reptile (cockatrice)—venomous, tyrannical, weak to seeing its own reflection

The Impulse: Climbing to the top and bragging about your "eggs" (achievements, status, procreation)

The Delusion: Confusing material worldly level with cosmic level—thinking you're on top of Heaven when you're just on top of a dunghill

The Polarity: Opposite of the Eagle of God (who flies under Heaven but above the World, seeing FOR God rather than BEING God)

Gender Expression:

In women: centered around procreation—bragging about laying eggs, making babies the ultimate achievement
In men: centered around dominance—being the alpha, conquering, accumulating status symbols
Simple Explanation:

Ever seen two pigeons fighting over a bread crumb when there are plenty of crumbs around? That's the Rooster of the World impulse—fighting to be ON TOP rather than being satiated.

It's why "successful" people are often the most miserable. They climbed the ladder thinking it reached Heaven, only to discover they're just louder roosters on a bigger dunghill.

The Rooster shrieks to announce darkness coming, not light.

Most "human elites" are just saurians who won the dinosaur game—and like the dinosaurs, they're a dead end. They distract humanity from what matters by making WINNING look like the point.

The sickness isn't ambition—it's confusing levels. Wanting to be on top of the WORLD (material) instead of working FOR God (cosmic).

Quick Test:

If your main source of identity comes from being "better than" others rather than serving something higher than yourself—congratulations, you're a rooster. 🐓👑💀
