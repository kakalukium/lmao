Try losing a small argument on purpose. It is the cheapest and fastest way to see who someone really is.

Hand someone a minor win and watch what they do with the short-lived power they attained. 

The gracious one will stay benevolent. 
The malicious one will show you their whole hand. The taste of influence usually leads them to drop their act.

You learn more from how someone wins than from how they lose.
