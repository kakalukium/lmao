Per Capita Income or National Income (Total Gross Domestic Product divided by the Total Population) has little to nothing to do with the Tech Landscape of a nation

Nations like Uruguay, Croatia, Slovakia have national incomes in the 25,000 to 30,000 Dollars range

Yet their Tech landscape remains largely similar and unchanged

Germany, Singapore and South Korea all saw their national income rise from 20,000 to 60,000 Dollars , 7,000 to 35,000 & 12,000 to 100,000 Dollars between 1990 and 2026

Yet changes in their technology landscape haven't been too revolutionary

They remain almost entirely reliant on American platforms and on American branded hardware in their digital ecosystems , with partnered core ownership in some areas such as Memory, Lithography etcetera

There isn't a German competitor to YouTube, Facebook or Amazon on a remotely comparable scale

There isn't a South Korean Operating System or a Singaporean Enterprise System that can operate Independently outside the American ecosystem

The only country whose tech landscape has changed drastically with national income has been China ☺

So this once again boils down to

Can India see a China like transformation if it's national income rises?

Question - Why don't prosperity and technology control go hand in hand?

![](https://qph.cf2.quoracdn.net/main-qimg-87444e920d80e7fa593885ef870e9807)

They do go hand in hand

All that happens is that - the Richer a nation becomes, the more it's population can afford applications & products running on advanced technology

This is Technology Access

The Richer a Nation, the more is the Technology Access

So India becoming richer, would mean more Indians buying IPhones, AI Tokens

It would mean more Indian services using expensive enterprise solutions for better delivery

It would mean a larger percentage of the population being able to download and become paid members on select digital platforms

It certainly does not mean India would be able to create or control technology

That is a wholly different ball game

Question - So does that mean you don't have to be rich to be able to create or control technology?

Yes

It does

Being Rich is certainly one area of eligibility

You can't control or create technology unless you have the necessary resources for financial control

However it is one of the most basic areas of eligibility

The analogy would be similar to saying

My son and daughter can be elected President of the States anytime after 2051 or 2054 respectively because they are Citizens

It is mandatory to be a Citizen for being elected President but even so the odds are astronomical of someone actually becoming the President, from the Citizen level up

In the same way

It is mandatory to be prosperous and rich to be able to create and control Technology but even so the odds of actually doing so are maybe 1 in a few hundred thousand.

Question - Why is China different ?

China has a separate system of Governance

One that had always placed it at loggerheads with the rest of the world

![](https://qph.cf2.quoracdn.net/main-qimg-aaffdcd3e271ffd618d068cbbb72f4b5)

Within China - the Communist Party is the Country

Despite the word ‘Party', the Communist Party of China is actually the equivalent of the Indian Parliament or American Congress

The G7 world has always seen this system as a threat right from the 1940s and as an Active threat since the 2020s

So as far as China is concerned - Technology Globalization is a ‘Vulnerability’ that could be exploited to weaken the Communist Party and hence the Chinese system

Self Reliance is not an Option

Self Reliance is a major necessity to preserve the strength and control of the Communist Party

So every dollar that China earns goes into Self Reliance & People

More Schools and Hospitals for the the People

More Parks and Recreation

More investments and subsidies into core technology

It is a combination of National Policy , National Resources & Leadership

Rising National Income is just an extra booster

Without rising national income, the Chinese would steal and reverse engineer

With rising national income, the Chinese would develop on their own through research

Technology Independence is of National Importance as a way to safeguard the entire Chinese system from vulnerability

Question - Does India have the same set of priorities?

Unequivocally and Resoundingly No

India is a Chaotic, Functional Democracy

Indians love the famous adage “Vox Populi Vox Dei”

India is not seen as a threat by anyone including Pakistan these days

India's system is not viewed with increasing suspicion

The States couldnt care less who the next Prime Minister of India would likely be

Technology Globalization is not seen as a ‘Vulnerability’ but rather as an Opportunity

Self reliance is not seen as a priority but rather as an uneconomical, very expensive, confrontational choice

![](https://qph.cf2.quoracdn.net/main-qimg-7c80c17a796e163acd2705bbc13129fd)

China is all about - We need a parallel ecosystem to ensure tomorrow we are not left dependent on G7 owned ecosystems which they could weaponize. So it is worth spending billions of dollars on developing our own parallel ecosystem. The entire system of China will be protected

India is all about - Why would we spend Billions of Dollars on developing a parallel ecosystem, risking confrontation with the States and the G7, invest years of effort when economically it makes little to no sense. Instead we can use this investment productively and collaborate with the G7 and make everything profitable for all
