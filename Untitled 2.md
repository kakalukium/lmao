Here are the facts

I. The Buyer booked a 34th Floor Apartment of 90 Sq m (970 Sft) the price of around 2,646 CNY per Sq M (Approximately around 2.34 Million CNY). This was in 2013!!!

II. He paid ONLY 5% of the price or 117,700 CNY

IV. The Builder refunded 50,000 CNY to him immediately and later 20,000 CNY - thus repaying 70,000 CNY

V. Unable to get 47,700 CNY, the Buyer approached the Public Consumer Forum in China who ORDERED the Builder to pay - 47,700 CNY refund IMMEDIATELY plus 27,000 CNY as interest in 2020

VI. The Builder paid 47,700 CNY to the buyer but has not yet paid the 27,000 CNY interest/compensation

The Buyer has got FULL REFUND of his money. Only the compensation part of 27,000 CNY is remaining.

Under OLD Chinese rules - If you don't deliver a home, you refund the ENTIRE DEPOSIT SUM PAID BY THE BUYER

Under NEW 2021 CHINESE RULES -If you don't deliver a home, you refund the ENTIRE DEPOSIT PAID BY THE BUYER PLUS AN ADDITIONAL 0.5% OF THE SUM FOR EVERY MONTH IT TOOK TO INFORM THE BUYER OF THE INABILITY TO DELIVER THE HOME

In this case , the delivery date was 2018 and the information came in 2017

So the consumer forum calculated 27,000 CNY compensation over 4 years

The Builder argued that THIS WAS BEFORE THE NEW RULES APPLIED SO A REFUND WAS SUFFICIENT

Now the case is under Consumer Appellate Forum
