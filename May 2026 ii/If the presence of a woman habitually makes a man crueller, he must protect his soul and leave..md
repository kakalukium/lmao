The issue is that divorce laws (the default "prenup") reflect an entirely different kind of relationship than modern so-called marriages.

The earlier marriage structure implies genuine sacrifice from both parties and specialization that could leave the woman vulnerable. More so, her contributions to the shared life entitled her to the assets built. That was the deal because neither of them had a separate life. They had one life... a shared life.

Modern marriages are often just two people who usually have fully separate lives and who just so happen to want to spent time together for a few years. The logic of the default shared property standard no longer applies. The woman doesn't need protection because hasn't sacrificed, nor has she helped him build anything that she has a defensible claim to.

Now of course, many people live out the traditional model and if they do, the default prenup is entirely acceptable.
