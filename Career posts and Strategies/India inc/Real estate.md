White money looking for "safety" goes into real estate. Black money looking for absorption goes into real estate. Parents looking for status go into real estate. Add to that NRIs with stronger dollars and dirhams now find the same overpriced Indian apartment suddenly less insane.

At best you can expect stagnation or small corrections in overheated pockets, but there will be no outright crash.
