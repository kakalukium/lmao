Bought a “premium” Godrej apartment in Gurgaon. 6+ months later, I wish someone had warned me.

If you’re considering buying a Godrej property, please read this before investing your hard-earned money.

We bought an apartment at Godrej, Gurugram, trusting the brand, its promises of premium construction and its much-advertised MIVAN technology.

Six-plus months later (after moving in), we are still dealing with what has been an absolute nightmare. ( I am not even including the delayed possesion ordeal here, possession was delayed by 2.5 years).

Persistent seepage: Our master bedroom continues to develop damp patches, with paint and plaster peeling off. After we moved in they had discovered that waste water from the upper floor was entering our house which they were dismissing as ‘normal’. 

Termite infestation: this is a ‘premium’ ‘new’ house and all our door frames were hollowed out by termites. 

Unexplained AC noises: Loud cracking and popping sounds continue despite repeated inspections and as per them ACs sounding like they’re short circuiting is also ‘normal’. They are not sharing the installed ACs documents/ invoices with us so we are not even able to register a complaint ourselves. The only response we get it that Godrej bulk purchased it so the team doesn’t have it. 

Water ingress near electrical installations and water running through electrical sockets is also ‘normal’ as per them. 

Complete silence from leadership: After repeatedly escalating our concerns, including to senior management, we received assurances of personal intervention. Now, nobody responds. Not the project team, not CRM, not senior leadership.

And this is what I find most disturbing: how can a developer sell homes marketed as premium, fail repeatedly to resolve defects, and then simply stop communicating with the people living in them? The monthly maintenance charged is Rs 25000 btw. 

I have spent over six months documenting everything. Hundreds of photographs, videos, emails and repeated requests for help. I would strongly caution prospective buyers against assuming that a recognised brand name guarantees construction quality or after-sales accountability.

Has anyone else with Godrej experienced persistent defects and a similar lack of response? How did you finally get them resolved?
