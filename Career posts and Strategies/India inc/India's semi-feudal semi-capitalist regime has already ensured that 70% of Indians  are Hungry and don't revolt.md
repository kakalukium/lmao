Every once in a while, this topic starts trending on India Twitter. Some Indian goes abroad, and is astounded and shocked out of their wits. And their reaction goes viral.

But the Indian regime isn't feeling exactly threatened. Only 1% of Indians have ever traveled outside India. Only 7% have passports. This cohort dominates online conversations and is the main target audience for advertisers (and hence the media) - but their overall numbers are too small to matter electorally. The fact that this crowd is often even more bigoted and ill-mannered than the "dehatis" they criticize is just the cherry on top.

India's semi-feudal semi-capitalist regime has already ensured that 70% of Indians don't get enough daily nutrition. Hungry people can't revolt after all. Or so the regime hopes.
