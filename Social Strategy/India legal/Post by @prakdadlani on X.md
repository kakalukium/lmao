Never work with an Indian manufacturer who does these 5 things.

They can cost you months of delays, bad products and lost money.

1\. Refuses third-party QC

They avoid factory audits or product inspections.

Walk away.

2\. Asks for payment to a personal account

Always pay the registered company account.

3\. Says “yes” to everything

No questions about specs, materials or timelines?

Red flag.

4\. Avoids contracts and POs

If everything is on WhatsApp, don't start production.

5\. Gives vague answers

Can't clearly explain their factory, samples or lead time?

Find another supplier.

A good manufacturer won't just say yes.

They'll tell you exactly how they will deliver.
