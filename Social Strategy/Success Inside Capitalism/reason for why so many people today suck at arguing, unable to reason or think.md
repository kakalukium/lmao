I think a reason for why so many people today suck at arguing, unable to reason or think, is because ‘thinking’ was never a requirement under agricultural society.

For thousands of years, the most socially and reproductively successful humans have been blind followers.

Thoughtless conformity was the gateway to a safe and comfortable life for the longest time.

Under this new society, the mentally inebriated peasants created a culture where moral indoctrination took the forms of fear (the threat of being condemned to hell by a wrathful God), righteous and dignified behaviour the form of shame (public stoning, lynching, slander, gossip, etc), and ambition realised through ulterior motives (faking a smile around the landowner improved your chances of not becoming the receiving-end to his whimsical behaviour and having your privileges to work on his field unfairly revoked).

Several hundreds of generations later, this has shaped the modern human to be obviously limbic, driven only by fear and arousal (proxying either a threat or opportunity).

The schools and social life more generally doesn’t help reverse this epidemic of thoughtlessness much either. Much of early schooling and childhood disciplining is achieved through fear, with the ideal of being a God-fearing human being who readily delegates his ‘thinking’ to authorities and tradition.

As such, the model citizen of this new world grows up ignorant, never having realised his own nature or that of the vast reality that lay outside his narrow gaze fixed by the most evolutionarily conserved imperatives. At the slightest suggestion of insult, provocation, or discomfort, he experiences a wild roller-coaster of contradictory and heightened emotions.

He shows little capacity for clear observance, fair judgement, or holistic and multi-faceted (also called systems) thinking. He is a simple creature of habit — reactive in a linear, self-serving way — making him easy to suggest, manipulate, and persuade as long as just the right type and amount of emotions can be roused.

Under this new rigid, oppressive, and dogmatic system birthed by the grain-fed, metabolically defected peasants (and later pastoralists), the man who is thoughtful, seeks depth of ambition, purpose, and thought, who seeks to organise his behaviour around higher principles and an ethos over mere opportunism, and who more so is not reactive to every little suggestion of social dishonour (shaming tactics and so) finds himself no place among the mentally ill circus of modern society.

Everything about this new world is designed to motivate a very questionable character — one who is vain, desires empty appearances, wishes to be admired or unquestioningly respected with no real grounds, and one who wants to make himself immune to all forms of moral scrutiny.

Since the inebriated peasants are not in possession of discriminating powers, they have collectively settled upon ceremonial rites of passage (hoop-jumping and qualifying rituals such as graduation or wedding ceremonies) to declare to the world what they want people to know about them. The acquisition of knowledge has to be declared. Love too has to be declared through pompous, tear-jerking festivities.

The more hypothyroid a population is, the more they lean toward ritualism and ceremonies as a way of easing off the cognitive load of making sense of what’s what. (I have illustrated this effect with the pastoral Masai, who are a violent, short-lived tribe that attain puberty precociously, and the foraging Hadzabe.)

Since social institutions, which are invariably shaped by the sentiments of the laymen, are to be the arbiters of competence and ‘usefulness’ under this new world, the man who is euthyroid is placed promptly smack at the bottom of the hierarchy, his talents now irrelevant to the circus.

The sheep-like men and women of agricultural society, by their sheer numbers, have created fertile grounds for an entirely new breed of men to rise and flourish among them — namely, charlatans, tricksters, con artists, and any one who can play the limbic drives of this now-devolved and atavistic man to his benefit.

In this cultural landscape, a clear sheep—shepherd dynamic emerges as the sheep-like humans, now unable to be a fair judge of character, place their confidence into anyone who can rouse their primitive emotions. The journalist cannot tell the difference between the one who is well-versed in a discipline vs. someone who’s using pointless verbosity and intimidating jargon to avoid public scrutiny. The elementary school teacher, in a similar vein, cannot tell apart a kid who’s upset because he’s been singled-out and bullied vs. the kids who’d actually done the bullying that are now crying crocodile tears to detract the inquiry.

In the modern cultural discourse, it is often said that the squeakiest wheel that gets the grease. Because in a culture where no one is ‘thinking’, the one who can cause the most overwhelming noise and timely distraction automatically takes all.

I have observed, time and again, that the men this society elevates and regards highly have always been of a particular bent of character, which I like to call the shepherd in this sheep—shepherd dynamic. To the ignorant population, the comforting illusion maintained by the spectacle, as described by the contemporary Marxist philosopher Guy Debord, satisfies the longing for sleep — to remain thoughtless, morally unaccountable, and clueless as to how to conduct life’s affairs. It is often these men that lend themselves in their duties to the upkeeping of the spectacle that are at-large considered intelligent, interesting, funny, caring, and adorned with any number of other endearing titles.

Make no mistake, it’s not like the more euthyroid among us cannot play the masses to buying ourselves rewards in this clown world of ours. We just don’t act on our knowledge because therein lies no fulfilment, in the kind of rewards we can obtain from the masses (their blind admiration, empty gestures, etc). (Do I really want friends who stick around because I service their vanity i.e. make them look good, giving them little crumbs of power in-exchange for propping up my façade?)

A ‘generative’ and creative life doesn’t sit well with the rigidity of the sheep—shepherd or follower—followed dynamic after all, unless you are looking to stagnate where you are, with what you are, looking to merely prolong the decay process of your being.

On the other hand, to the simplistic man of pleasure, society is his hedonistic playground, and he has every reason to feel absolutely enthralled by the system in place.

From my time on Quora, I’ve gathered that even for an international audience, we are an embarrassingly stupid species where we stand today ..and sometimes, just like that, it’s wiser for men like me to not fight or “prove ourselves” when challenged by members of the circus. To find and finesse your brand of independent wealth creation, keep a healthy distance from mainstream society, and curate a tightly-knit, little community of gamer girls and artsy emo bitches is what I’ve learned — we do not belong with the monkeys!
