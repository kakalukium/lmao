
Human stupidity is.

Take your average Joe and leave him to his own resourcefulness and intelligence to make sense of and overcome an obstacle.

Give him all the resources he will ever need to tackle the problem, access to information and such.

If he cannot, that is to say if he finds himself looking out to others, calling up his friends, to help him along and walk him through his internal monologue, or worse yet, indebts himself to a scam artist who promises to perform his due diligence for him- Then there’s your problem.

![[images/08ea394312b49febd684856fab171dd5_MD5.jpg]]

When enough people in a society become stupid in this way, as they did during the turn of the Neolithic revolution, you find them needing someone to organise and direct them towards actions that would serve them and larger society.

Since the average peon lacks the capacity for self-directed inquiry or learning, that is to make himself amount to anything worthy of himself under his own watch, he elects in leaders who promise to do all the hard work for him towards organising an economy.

Under this economy, he will serve as a cog in the machine, a specialised insect that will take exacting instructions and carry them out pedantically to the dot with no variation in the order of duties.

His masters will be responsible for the conceptual and organisational aspects of his life, from stipulating how the land or factory floor be allocated, to which machines or crops, to ultimately giving him a role to perform, so he can be of ‘use’ to himself and others.

Over time, such a dynamic hands over executive power to a handful, leading to the stratification of society along the lines of class.

The masters, now with all the power they could ask for, go unchecked in enacting their whims and desires onto the general populace, who are anything but moronic and whose choices were the root cause of their troubles down the line.

Of course, the short-sighted peons will not allow themselves to become receptive to the truth of the matter. Everyone evaluates reality only as deeply as their self-awareness goes.

An individual who lacks self-awareness will readily feel victimised by all things that do not turn out in his favour, because he cannot contrive of a reason for why his intended action did not align with the outcome. If and when things work out for him, he will resort to pride, without stopping to think of how he could've achieved his success, or whether it can be reproduced sustainably before his luck runs out.

When you’re a hypothyroid, high-serotonin grain-feeder, your primary cognitions are of a limbic bent, taking form from a place of scarcity (fear and greed), with the reality you materialise all around you being a late-order consequence of your actions enacted from within your attained level of consciousness.

Take for example, relationships.

If you’re operating from a place of fear, you will select partners out of a ‘fear of missing out’, because it looks like a ‘race’ to see who can settle down and have kids the earliest.

The peasants, lacking any strong self-concepts (of who they are, what they truly want, etc), are only capable of structuring their ambitions around immediate wants and needs (such as the need for social validation).

In enacting their actions out of implied scarcity, even if there is none in reality, they are locking themselves into unfulfilling relationships.

Because they are thinking from a place of greed, every bit of effort spared towards an outcome that hasn’t beared fruit is seen as a ‘loss’ that must be redeemed, somehow. This, to the wise, is known as the sunken cost fallacy.

So, once they’ve committed themselves out of fear, they keep themselves addicted to any of its menaces because “the effort has already been put forth” into creating a union.

The peasants, as such, cannot break old habits, un-learn their conditionings, or learn for that matter. They live their whole lives playing a broken record, showing little acumen towards lifelong learning and adaptation.

They become addicted to rituals, superstitions, traditions, and whatever else soothes their limbic imperatives without ever bringing themselves to seeing eye-to-eye with reality.

Since their motivation derives from the same limbic imperatives, they fall into apathy and indifference should the stressful reminders of, say, an impending divorce or a missed re-payment be removed.

It is only under this state of constant threat does he find the impetus to stay on his good behaviour and remain ‘in his senses’, much like your average hypothyroid, estrogen-dominant woman who starts misbehaving when you treat her nicely or give her a stable home free of drama.

Coming back to our central point, capitalism is only corruptible if the people are.

If your people are discerning, self-sufficient, and diligent citizens, who each come into this world in possession of differentiating talents, then you can do away with leadership altogether.

After all, what good is a shepherd to a self-aware population that knows how to take care of themselves without infringing upon the rights of others, with the capacity to understand and work alongside one another to mutual benefit?

For as long as you have a sheep-like population, you will always need to pawn yourself to the capitalistic class to enjoy even a bare minimum standard of living.

Communism is an utopic pipe dream. Utopias only work under utopic conditions i.e. where the people are worthy of such an abundant reality of automated surplus production and creative expression for all.

When your average dingus cannot contrive a meaningful and original essay despite his formal education, only able to bastardise from other sources, he is only good for slavery, and a society that thrives under an oppressive leash.
