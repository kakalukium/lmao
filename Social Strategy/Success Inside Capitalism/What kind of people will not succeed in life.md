The latter, more unfortunate archetype would be someone with a high intelligence and morals that equally measures up to their intellectual fortitude. People of this nature often cultivate and abide by an ethos that’s independent of their social environment. Someone like this will be frowned upon not just for their unorthodox ways to conducting life’s usual affairs but moreover the sheer contempt they display for the status quo. This combination almost certainly spells disaster. But why?

If it didn’t benefit the herd by some arbitrary chance, it will be made to over due course of time. Systems are ultimately shaped by the majority species in it. The average folk have the option of conformity the intelligent on the contrary, simply don’t. While the less intelligent able to align their natural proclivities with the collective, the intelligent aren’t. To them, conformity becomes an exercise in renouncing themselves and all their individualism. Emulating the tribe’s every behavioural nuance comes at a unreasonably high mental and even physical cost - often amounting to unbearable stress resulting in a reduced sense of wellness over time. In other words, attempting to fit in might as well set you up on a path to an early grave.
