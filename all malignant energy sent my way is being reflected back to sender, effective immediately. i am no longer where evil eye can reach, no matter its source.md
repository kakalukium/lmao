a simple reflective spell for beginners:

write down the name of the person who’s targeting you, or a general addressor like "anyone who redirects harmful energy to my field", on a piece of paper. then, place a small mirror inside a jar or container, and in front of the mirror, place the open written paper. 

state your intention out loud, clearly and as many times as you’d like. something like: "all malignant energy sent my way is being reflected back to sender, effective immediately. i am no longer where evil eye can reach, no matter its source. the charge is now returned at double the strength it came in.”

then close the jar to seal the incantation. that simple. the sender is now facing their own reflection continuously. put it away somewhere dark and undisturbed like a closet or a drawer, and do not revisit it or check on it. set it and trust your power, then see how things that were blocked simply begin to move for you!
