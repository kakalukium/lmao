### SSD Upgrade Advantages
- **Multiple M.2 Slots**: Most Z890 boards in this range offer **4+ M.2 connectors** (at least 1–2 PCIe 5.0). You can start with one 1–2TB boot drive and easily add more for games, media, or scratch disks.
- **Future-Proof**: Supports high-speed Gen5 SSDs (up to 14,000+ MB/s) for fast loading/transfer.
- **No Compromises**: Full x16 PCIe 5.0 for the GPU even with multiple SSDs populated.

**Total Estimated Power**: Still ~450–600W under load — very efficient.

### Additional Recommendations
- **Extra SSDs to Consider Later**: Add another 2TB Gen4/Gen5 drive (~$100–200) when needed. Many boards include heatsinks for sustained SSD performance.
- **OS & Software**: Windows 11 + latest BIOS/drivers for best compatibility.
