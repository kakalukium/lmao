### Key Architectural Highlights for Expansion:
 * **Massive Storage Upgrade Path:** The chosen ATX B650 motherboards (like the Tomahawk or Aorus Elite) feature **3x M.2 NVMe slots**. This means you can drop in your primary 1TB Gen4 drive today and still have two completely unpopulated M.2 slots to easily add higher-capacity NVMe SSDs later on without needing to swap anything out.
 * **Streamlined RAM Upgrades:** Starting with **1x 16GB stick** leaves **3 open DIMM slots** on your board. When you want to step up to 32GB or more, you simply have to purchase the exact same matching stick of G.Skill 16GB 6000MHz RAM and snap it into the secondary slot to enable Dual-Channel performance.
 * **The Blackwell Advantage:** The **RTX 5060 8GB** delivers next-generation architecture featuring **GDDR7 memory**, blazing-fast AI processing capabilities, and exclusive access to **DLSS 4 Multi Frame Generation**.
 * **Uncompromising Processing Power:** Pairing it with the **Ryzen 7 7800X3D** guarantees that your PC is entirely bottleneck-free. The massive 3D V-Cache handles high frame rates, simulation physics, and heavy background workloads effortlessly.
