**Paras Chopra** @paraschopra [2025-09-10](https://x.com/paraschopra/status/1965635343878947208)

If you want to get into AI research, don’t start with LLMs.

Start with fundamentals: statistics, linear algebra, calculus, machine learning theory, optimization and probability.

You’ll quickly realise that

\- there’s no magic sauce, and

\- how all learning is smoothly interpolating around training data, and

\- how different architectures simply give us inductive biases for efficient convergence to an optima

---

**Paras Chopra** @paraschopra [2025-09-10](https://x.com/paraschopra/status/1965664187029532852)

You can view many ML/AI methods as data generating mechanisms.

• Diffusion: add noise to data point to find out what points lie near noise

• RL: generate data from environment to know what actions are beneficial

---

**Paras Chopra** @paraschopra [2025-09-10](https://x.com/paraschopra/status/1965664229815701529)

While we constrain LLMs to existing data (mostly), humans seem to be masters at generating their own data.

Give someone rules of chess and they would simulate a chess game in their head and learn from that simulation!

Our dreams and imagination is literally data-creation
