What's good when you sacrifice yourself for them? Nothing. So stop doing it. They don't deserve it. Because if they did deserve it, they would never let you sacrifice yourself.

The right people refuse your sacrifice. They insist on meeting you halfway. They feel uncomfortable taking more than they give. But the ones who let you bleed yourself dry, who accept everything you offer without question, without guilt, they were never worth it to begin with.

Stop funding people with your energy, your time, your peace. The return will never come. And you already know that.

###### Stay away from immature people.

Not because they are bad. But because immaturity is expensive. It costs you time, energy, and peace.

They cannot handle conflict without making it personal. They cannot take responsibility without deflecting it onto someone else first.

And over time, you start shrinking your own standards just to keep the peace.

Stay away. Not with anger. With clarity. Because your growth requires an environment that can hold it.
