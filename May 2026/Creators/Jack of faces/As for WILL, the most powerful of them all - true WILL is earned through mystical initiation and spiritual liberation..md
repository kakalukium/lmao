No one is tough.

One minute of continuous physical activity and the average male is wheezing and wailing and hopelessly out of breath, regardless of size or stature.

One single minute of punching air is enough.

Physical toughness is earned through preparation, only a broken body can house an unbreakable mind.

Mental toughness, on the other hand, is earned through difficult living, and facing your life's lessons boldly and fearlessly.

There is no such thing as a weak person, only those who avoid the tests of life (and God), and those who dare to take them on.

God will perfect you.

You are his instrument in this world.

He will sharpen your very soul so that you may cut through the lies of this world.

As for WILL, the most powerful of them all - true WILL is earned through mystical initiation and spiritual liberation.

WILL is what can take a man to places he was almost never destined to go.

It goes beyond physical discipline, mental toughness and temperament.

Will transcends both body and mind.

It is a thing of the spirit.
