If you've followed my page for a while now, you will know that I teach about the hidden aspect.

What is the hidden aspect?

The hidden aspect is an inherent energetic vacuum, so to speak, that every single thing carries in the material world.

It is the unseen effect of an energetic clash. The space that is made when the quantum materials of reality become overly excited.

A thing that becomes created both naturally and inadvertently. An uninformed consequence of global or nation-wide occurrences.

Remember this rule, the ones living the best are always living in quietude.

The best places, the best cuisine, the best entertainment, the best relationships, the best vacations, the best living conditions, the best of anything - is hidden and rarely ever openly talked about.

If it's advertised, it's not it.

You don't get to the end of the rainbow by aggressively pursuing it, you do so with a little hint from a friend, or by eavesdropping in peculiar places or some desperate corner-street that just feels out of touch for most people.

If there's everyone around it - it's not it.

If there's no one around it - it's not it either.

These are things and places that exist beyond the attention and movements of the masses, but not wholly out of sight - somewhere in the peripherals of life.

In a world that is seemingly very loud and noisy and chaotic (and this is currently its most potent illusion) you must know that there are equally mystical things happening very quietly - in contrast to that chaos, behind the scenes.

When you are armed with the knowledge of this principle - you can never truly unsee the secret pathways made available to you through simple observation.

I call this the Art of Quiet Living.

I have based my life, more or less, on this principle.

And it has taken me, and continues to take me, to incredible places.

Your participation in the arena of life, be it political, or military, or ideological - tunes your energy into these channels.

Once you have attuned your psyche and energy to these frameworks, you fall victim to the lens of their limited reality.

But the moment you understand that the clock leads to the mechanism - and the mechanism leads to the backstage, and the backstage leads to the architect - you will gradually learn to know which paths to take and which to ignore.

The starting point of this practice is the careful and deliberate removal - not of your attention, not of your energy - but of your participation in the global energetic exchange.

Even this initial clue or information you're gaining - is not through some Hollywood grade podcast or ultra popular self-help book, right?

But rather through "shoddy and shady" means.

From a stranger in an unlikely corner of the internet.

For you to even be here, you have, to a degree, must have already ceased participation.

Or have at least entertained the idea.

The rest is up to discernment.
