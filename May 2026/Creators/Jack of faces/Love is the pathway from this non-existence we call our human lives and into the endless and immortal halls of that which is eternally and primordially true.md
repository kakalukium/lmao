Yes, nothing transcends this world but love.

When a person passes - notice how whatever attaches them and their spirit to this world is a list of their emotions.

Grievances, hurt, pain, vengeance, disappointment, unfinished businesses.

It is never love, though their loved ones remain here on this Earth.

It is never love that attaches their spirit to this place.

But it is love through which they are sent to their final resting place.

Because the final resting place is love.

On one side, a constant awaiting of reuniting with those we have connected our spirits with through love - a force that which transcends time, place and even circumstance.

On the other, an inevitable homecoming into the warm and gentle embrace of those who we know love us.

Even this world, the whole of our physical-material existence, was created for love.

So that those removed from the graces heaven and those under its blessings could meet each other again, in this strange and difficult place, because they were, at one point, connected through love.

It is this love that keeps the spirit immortal.

So long as one is loved - they are not without a guardian.

They are not beyond redemption.
