I have realised one of the biggest advantages you can build in life is becoming incredibly hard to distract from what you have decided matters. Not motivated.Not permanently “locked in.” just genuinely difficult to separate from the thing you have chosen; There is a massive difference.

Most people do not lose because they are incapable. They lose because their attention is continuously being auctioned off in tiny pieces; notifications, conversations, other people's opinions, random opportunities, emotional fluctuations, meaningless information. Nothing destroys you dramatically. You just slowly become less concentrated.

I think this is one of the reasons deep work has changed me so much. When you sit with the same problem for hours, days, sometimes weeks, something begins happening which cannot happen when you keep breaking the surface of your attention. The problem starts opening. You begin seeing the second layer. Then the third.

You notice contradictions you couldn't see before. Strange connections appear. Sentences arrive which contain far more than you initially understood. Something you thought was one problem suddenly reveals itself as five interacting mechanisms.

This is where I think a lot of genuine excellence comes from not simple intelligence; but something closer to the depth of contact. Most people touch an idea and move on. The person attempting to become exceptional stays there long enough for the idea to begin speaking back.

There is also something deeply enjoyable about living this way. You stop needing constant stimulation because the thing directly in front of you becomes interesting enough. Your work gets deeper. Your thoughts get cleaner. You develop this beautiful relationship with your own mind where you know if something feels unfinished you can remain with it until you understand what is bothering you. That produces an enormous amount of self trust. I am increasingly convinced that some of the highest forms of intelligence are simply unavailable to the chronically interrupted person. This is why everyone is a moron now, social media, distractions, a million notifications everyday

And then when you find something real, you attack it with force.

That is the synthesis.
