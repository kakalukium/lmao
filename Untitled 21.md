Question : What do the protestors want?

The CJP was formed because a Judge in India derisively referred to protestors as “Cockroaches”

So are the CJP against Judicial Apathy?

The NEET paper was leaked. The re examination has been conducted , without any further allegations of leaks or any instances of alleged mismanagement / fraud. So is this not the case of rectifying errors?

I can understand if the Government refused to conduct a re examination. If they stuck to their guns. They didn't. They admitted a leak and they have done what anyone else would have done

So are the CJP against the Government doing fair by the students and holding a re examination?

A Case was registered against the perpetrators of the fraud and to this day I see no allegations of judicial malfeasance or rigged investigation

So are the CJP protesting against the Government for attempting to bring the perpetrators of the paper leaks to justice?

The entire core premise of the CJP protests appear to be along the lines of “We hate the Government and the BJP” rather than against any clear line of action taken by the Government
