Indian fathers are destroying their Gen Z sons’ marriages in the name of the Great Indian Family Business.

They love complaining that the new generation is entitled and running away from the family business. They are running from a system designed to completely emasculate them.

Humiliating realities of a second generation Director in a traditional Indian business.

1\. A 27 year old married man working 10 hours a day alongside his father. But he draws zero actual market salary because "ghar ke kharche papa chala rahe hai" (Dad is paying the household bills).

2\. When his wife needs money, he has to awkwardly ask his father for cash.

3\. When his own child’s school fee is due, the grandfather signs the cheque.

4\. He cannot even book a 3 day weekend vacation with his wife without pitching it to his dad like a corporate budget approval.

5\. The father vetoes every single attempt to modernize the business as he gives his son zero actual financial authority and then sits among his friends and complains that his boy is lethargic and has lost interest.

A fully grown married adult who has been completely helpless in his own home. His wife is miserable watching her husband act like a dependent teenager and his confidence is entirely shattered.

They think they are building an empire to pass down. In reality, they are weaponizing dependency to maintain control over their bloodline.

You cannot expect a child to modernize an empire when you won't even let them control their own wallet.

A family business that pays you in comfort instead of a salary is just financial castration. Don’t confuse this with inheritance.
