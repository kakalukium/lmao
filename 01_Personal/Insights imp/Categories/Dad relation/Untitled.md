One of the most costly mistakes a man can make is to accept responsibility without demanding the authority that comes with it.

The day you are expected to bear the consequences without being able to influence the decisions that produce them, you cease to be an actor.

You become a fuse. A handy tool used to absorb the pressure while others retain the power.

In any organization, responsibility and authority must evolve together.

If one increases without the other, someone is transferring the risk… and that someone is probably not you.
