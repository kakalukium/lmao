Low-agency and high-agency people have different priors about the malleability of reality.

Low-agency people have a prior that reality is fixed and the world has rules. Things are the way they are and your job is to navigate the world as it exists. When you encounter a barrier, that's the world telling you "this path is closed." You respect that. You find an open path. If all paths are closed, you wait for someone to open one.

High-agency people have a prior that reality is negotiable. The world has defaults, not rules. Things are the way they are because no one has changed them yet. Your job is to shape the world toward what you want. When you encounter a barrier, that's just the world's opening offer. You make a counteroffer. You negotiate. You find or create leverage. The path isn't open? Make it open.
