What is there to gain from magic?

Magic has pretty much changed my life in every way imaginable. In very very harsh and grueling ways, but overall it has greatly benefited my existence. My reality operates in unquestionably different ways than it did prior to me finding the occult, but I’m unsure of what it is exactly that I’ve gained from it. And I somehow also still couldn’t tell you what magic even is

Is it some sort of secret knowledge about the universe itself or is it a deeper understanding of how your mind works, like an expanded perception? Or a mix of the two? I feel way more content with my life because of what I’ve learned from magic but I genuinely don’t know what it is that I’ve actually learned. And I’m unsure of why I ever decided to dive into the occult in the first place, almost like I can’t even remember the specific moment when I first did, other than the year and roughly the month. But I’m glad I did
