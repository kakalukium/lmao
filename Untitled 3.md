What I'd change with the remaining budget
Your budget is actually high for an RTX 5060 build.
Instead of spending ₹1.5L on aesthetics, I'd consider:
RTX 5060 → RTX 5070
Keep Ryzen 5 9600X
Keep 32GB DDR5-6000
Keep B650 WiFi board
The GPU is usually the biggest gaming bottleneck. An RTX 5070 would give a much larger performance increase than upgrading the CPU further. The RTX 5060 is primarily a strong 1080p card, though it can handle many games at 1440p with DLSS. Its 8GB VRAM can become a limitation in some newer AAA titles at higher settings and resolutions. �
