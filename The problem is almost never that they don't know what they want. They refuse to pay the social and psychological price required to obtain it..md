Most people don't fear mediocrity. They fear the price to pay to escape it: Rejection, embarrassment, failure seen by others, judgment. Instability, coming across as arrogant, Being misunderstood.

So they choose stagnation, because it is socially acceptable. The problem is almost never that they don't know what they want. They refuse to pay the social and psychological price required to obtain it.
