I call this “garden theory”

you’re better off building gardens than chasing butterflies

both can get you butterflies. but when chasing fails, you end up tired. when gardening fails, you still end up with a better garden

every hour spent improving your craft, character, network, health, or reputation makes the garden richer

every hour hunting for a specific opportunity disappears the moment you miss it

applies to jobs, dating, friends, investors, networking, and anything where you’re being filtered out and chosen

in markets where you’re being selected, your best strategy is usually to become more selectable

so people will start coming to you, and you end up being the chooser instead of the beggar

in finance/engineering we call this “convexity,” where actions have asymmetric upside and bounded downside

in this case, even if nobody picks you today, you’ve still increased your value tomorrow
