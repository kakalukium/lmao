Ask an addict if there is such a concept as free will and they will likely say no. They move through life feeling as if all their will has been ceded to their environment. An addict is at the mercy of hundreds of cues (time of day, places, moods) all of which trigger a powerful spike of dopamine that compels them to pursue their vice. And every time they follow the spike, they cede another portion of their will to their environment. 

Ask a successful entrepreneur if there is such a concept as free will and they will likely say yes. But the entrepreneur may have also ceded their will to their environment, just like the addict. The difference between them is that an addict’s environment pulls them in a direction counter to their higher values. An entrepreneur’s environment has been crafted over time to pull them in a direction that aligns with their higher values.[RaccoonsUnleashed](https://x.com/RaccoonUnleash)[@RaccoonUnleash](https://x.com/RaccoonUnleash)
