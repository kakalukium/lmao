If root themes are unstable, consciousness fixates on survival, compulsive desire, addiction, hypervigilance, or control

As stability develops, energy naturally rises:

Sacral → pleasure, sexuality, creativity, emotional flow

Solar plexus → willpower, confidence, individuality

Heart → love, compassion, connection

Throat → authentic expression

Third eye → insight, intuition, symbolic perception

Crown → transcendence, unity, spirituality

This is why many people try to “ascend” spiritually while their nervous system is still dominated by fear and instability

The energy cannot sustainably rise because the foundation is not embodied, leading to states of dissociation and disembodiment

The psyche climbs the pyramid in the same way kundalini rises through the energy centers: through increasing integration, safety, consciousness, and embodiment
